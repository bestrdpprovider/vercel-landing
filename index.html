How reliable is the uptime?
We provide 99.99% guaranteed uptime. We deliberately keep a maximum of 12 users per server and never overload our nodes, so your desktop stays fast and online around the clock.

You can choose from the USA, UK (London), and Netherlands. Our USA Super RDP plan gives you a choice of five locations across the USA.
How fast will I get access after paying?
Setup is quick. Your remote desktop is usually ready within 1 hour, and within a maximum of 4 hours, depending on staff availability. As soon as it is ready, you receive your login details and can connect right away. Our support team is available 24/7 if you need a hand.
Are my payments and data secure?
Yes. All payments are handled over secure SSL, and your remote desktop runs on pure SSD servers we manage and maintain. With 13+ years in business and 50,000+ satisfied customers, security and reliability are at the core of what we do.
Who is RDP Arena built for?
Our remote desktops are a great fit for freelancers, forex traders, developers, businesses, marketers, and anyone who needs a fast, always-on Windows machine in the cloud. Service is intended for legal business and personal productivity use only.
